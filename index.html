How the count works
  1. In TikTok Ads Manager, open Reporting and run a custom report for the week (Monday to Sunday) with Video material ID, Ad name and Conversions (or Purchases). Export it as CSV or XLSX.
  2. Import it here on that week. Each row is matched to a creator by a video ID you've assigned, or by the creator's name tag in the video or ad name.
  3. Conversions are the purchases TikTok attributes to each video under the ad account's attribution settings.
  4. So matching is automatic: name every creator's file with their tag before it goes into the Creative Library (HANNA_hook1_9x16.mp4), and keep one creator per ad. An ad that mixes two creators can only be split with the per-video export.
